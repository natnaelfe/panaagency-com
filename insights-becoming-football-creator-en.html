How Do I Become a Football Creator? The Path to Your First Brand Deals

Find your topic, build a community, become visible to brands and join an agency network.

In short

You become a football creator by regularly publishing content on a clear topic, building a community and being easy for brands to find. Brand deals come from engagement, content quality and fit with the scene, not from follower count alone.

By PANA Agency · Updated: October 16, 2026

Start with a topic and a platform

Pick a clear focus, such as tactics, goalkeeping, amateur football, fan culture or challenges. Choose a platform where you can deliver regularly: Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. Those who publish consistently and recognizably are more likely to become part of the scene.

What brands want to see

  • Engagement: How actively does your community respond?
  • Content quality: Do style and standard fit brands?
  • Fit with the football community: Do you come across as authentic in the scene?
  • Reliability: Keeping to deadlines, briefings and approvals.
  • Clear audience: Brands want to know who you reach.

From channel to first brand deal

  1. Compile your numbers: Reach, engagement and audience in a short media kit.
  2. Find fitting brands: Products you really use and that suit your community.
  3. Clarify the conditions: Agree service, fee, usage rights and duration up front.
  4. Implement the briefing: Follow the guardrails, submit content for approval, label advertising.
  5. Share the results: Numbers after the campaign are the best reference for the next deal.

When an agency makes sense

As soon as inquiries take more time than the content itself, an agency helps with brokering, negotiation and handling. What a management takes over and what to look out for is explained in the article Do I need a management as a creator?

Apply to PANA

Through the application form you send us your Instagram or TikTok channel and a few key details. We look not only at follower count but at engagement, content quality and fit with the football community. Joining the roster is free, exclusivity is not a requirement, and we usually get back to you within a few working days.
