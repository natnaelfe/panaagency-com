How much does influencer marketing in sports cost?

Budget, pricing factors and compensation models for campaigns with sports and football creators.

In short

Our recommendation for creator marketing in sports is a monthly influencer budget of 5,000 to 10,000 euros, so that campaigns deliver meaningful results. If you want to test first, you can also start with a smaller budget. The exact price depends on the number of creators, the duration and the scope of deliverables.

By PANA Agency · Updated: October 14, 2026

What determines the price?

There is no fixed price per campaign. These factors determine the budget:

  • Number of creators: The more creators involved, the higher the overall effort.
  • Reach and community: Larger and more engaged communities justify higher fees.
  • Platform and format: A story is different from a short video or a YouTube integration.
  • Scope of deliverables: How much content is produced and how elaborate is the production?
  • Duration: A single wave is priced differently than a campaign over several months.
  • Usage rights: If the content is to be used on your own channels or in ads, this is negotiated.
  • Exclusivity: Excluding competitors is a price factor of its own.
  • Approvals and compliance: Regulated industries need more coordination.

Which compensation models are there?

  • Fixed fee per piece of content or package: The classic, easy to plan.
  • Long-term collaboration: Several waves with one creator build recognition.
  • Products or benefits in kind: For suitable offers, such as tickets, an exchange can make sense.
  • Guaranteed reach: Possible on request, depending on the briefing and the approval process. We clarify this upfront in the contract and look for a solution that works for everyone.

How to plan your budget

  1. Set the goal: Reach, traffic, leads or awareness call for different formats.
  2. Choose the period: A one-off activation or an ongoing collaboration?
  3. Define the creator mix: A few fitting creators can achieve more than many random ones.
  4. Plan a buffer: Good campaigns evolve. At SOCCERBEAT the scope was expanded from 5 to 13 pieces of content during the campaign.
  5. Measure success: Define up front how success is measured and report after each wave.
