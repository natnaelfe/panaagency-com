Creator marketing in regulated industries: how approvals and compliance work

Approval process, labeling and briefing for regulated products, with examples from practice.

In short

It works with a clear approval process: statements, mandatory information and labeling are agreed between client, agency and creator before publication. PANA has run campaigns for a leading neobroker including BaFin-relevant approvals.

By PANA Agency · Updated: October 14, 2026

Note: This article describes our practice and is not legal advice. Which rules apply in a specific case is for the advertising company to clarify with its legal or compliance department.

Why regulated industries are different

For financial products and other regulated offers, statements cannot be chosen freely. Creators are used to working in their own style. Here, the creator's style has to fit the requirements for content and labeling.

What typically needs to be agreed

  • Approved core statements: What may be said and what must not be said?
  • Mandatory information and notices: Which information has to appear in the content or the description?
  • Advertising labeling: Collaborations must be clearly recognizable as advertising.
  • Dos and don'ts: A clear briefing prevents later rounds of corrections.
  • Approval before publication: Content is reviewed and approved before it is posted.

How the process works

  1. Clarify guardrails: The client names the requirements of its compliance team.
  2. Brief the creator: The briefing translates them into understandable rules for the creator.
  3. Submit drafts: The creator submits content for approval.
  4. Approve or correct: Client and agency approve it or request changes.
  5. Publish and report: After the rollout, reporting follows.

Examples from practice

For the World Cup 2026 campaign for a leading neobroker, all BaFin-relevant approvals and labeling requirements were taken into account across 39 pieces of content from four creators. The campaign for the retirement savings account was briefed and approved in full compliance with BaFin requirements. Both are described in our case studies.

Combining speed and compliance

Especially for time-critical campaigns it pays to define the approval process early: with fixed contacts, clear response deadlines and a briefing that avoids follow-up questions. Approvals take time, so they should be planned from the start.
