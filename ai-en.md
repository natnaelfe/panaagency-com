## Services for brands

### 1. Full Service Creator Management (most popular)

End-to-end execution: strategy, creator selection, briefing, negotiation, campaign management, compliance and performance reporting. One fixed point of contact.

Process:
1. **Starting point:** Intro call, goals, budget framework, target audience and success metrics.
2. **Creator matching:** Data-based selection of sport creators by goal, audience and platform. If the network is not enough, PANA also researches creators outside it.
3. **Campaign:** Briefing, content approval, compliance and publishing.
4. **Reporting:** Transparent tracking after each campaign wave (reach, engagement, conversions) with clear learnings.
5. **Scaling:** What works gets expanded: more creators, more platforms, long-term partnerships.

### 2. Strategy Consulting

PANA develops a positioning in sport and a cross-channel strategy that the brand's own team then executes.

Process:
1. **Analysis:** The brand's current position, audience and existing sport activities.
2. **Market analysis:** Relevant leagues, clubs, formats and competitions, who is already active and where a credible new voice fits.
3. **Positioning:** A clear position in sport that sets the brand apart.
4. **Strategy and channels:** Channel, format and partnership strategy as a playbook.
5. **Roadmap:** Timeline with milestones and success metrics for the first months.
6. **Ongoing support:** Weekly reporting and strategy calls.

Entry point: free analysis call.

### Difference between the two

Full Service covers complete execution from creator matching to reporting. Strategy Consulting delivers positioning and strategy; the brand's team handles execution.

## Budget and terms

- **Recommendation:** €5,000 to €10,000 influencer budget per month for meaningful results.
- **Smaller start possible:** for example a test with one or two creators.
- **Cost depends on:** number of creators, duration and scope of deliverables.
- **Budget proposals:** PANA works with packages and budget ranges.
- **Reach guarantees:** possible on request, depending on the briefing and approval process.
- **Intro call:** free, including a first budget estimate.
- **Timeline:** After the intro call and creator matching, first campaigns are usually live within a few weeks. Reactive campaigns around current events can start much faster.

## For creators

PANA manages sport creators and brokers brand deals.

- **Creator Management:** legal and organizational support, briefings, content guidelines and timing, contract and fee negotiations.
- **Brand Deals:** finding the right partnerships, handling the full process, negotiating terms and usage rights, long-term collaborations.
- **Personal Brand Development:** sharpening positioning, content and platform strategy, formats based on each creator's USP and trends.

Terms for creators:
- Joining the network is free. PANA earns from brokering and handling brand deals.
- No minimum follower count. PANA looks at engagement, content quality and fit with the sport community.
- Exclusivity is not required and is discussed individually.
- Pay is per brand deal, negotiated individually and agreed before commitment.

Apply: submit your channel (Instagram or TikTok) via https://pana-agency.de/creator.html#bewerbung or email hallo@pana-agency.de. Response usually within a few business days.
